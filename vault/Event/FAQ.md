# FAQ

Restated in our own words from the portal [P].

## Who can take part

- Active-duty and reserve Navy and Marine Corps personnel of any designator, rating or rank, plus other service members.
- DoW government civilians, contractors, academia and industry.
- Industry partners are accepted based on submitted use cases and problem statements, and this is not a product demo venue. Industry may also register to support hacking teams.
- Two roles: active hacker, or observer and evaluator.
- Participation is subject to command approval. The terms add citizenship and age requirements; see [[Event/Terms]].

## What to expect

- Join a team and build across all three days. Come committed to the whole event, since there is no virtual option.
- Coding experience is not required. Operational experience from the CIC, bridge and deckplates is essential.
- The event is unclassified.

## Registration and logistics

- Registering does not guarantee a place. Approved participants get a confirmation email.
- Venue, access, hotel and IT details go to confirmed participants.
- Commands may issue TAD or TDY orders under the Joint Travel Regulations. Coordinate authorization and funding with your command.

## Dress

- Active duty: uniform of the day (NWU, khakis or service equivalent).
- Everyone else: casual to business casual.

## Connectivity

- Wireless is available, and wired access is limited. For wired, bring a CAT6 patch cable and an Ethernet adapter if needed.
- No personal hotspots, wireless printers or other wireless network gear at the venue. See [[Event/Venue-and-IT]].
