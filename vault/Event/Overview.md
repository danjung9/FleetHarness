# Overview

## What it is

- [P] Fleethacker brings Sailors, Marines, engineers and technologists together to prototype Super Intelligence solutions quickly, on challenges that come from the Surface Force. Focus areas include cognitive burden, shipboard workflows and edge SI in disconnected environments.
- [P] Portal event name: Norfolk. Brand: FLEETHACKER. Sponsored by CDAO and the Department of the Navy. Listed organizer: Task Force Hopper. Event messages are signed by the planning cell of Task Force Hopper and Defense Unicorns.
- [P] Organizers encourage bringing existing tools and ideas. Teams do not have to start from scratch, but rule 7 applies. See [[Sprint/Prior-Work-Log]].
- [P] Unclassified, in person only, with no virtual component.
