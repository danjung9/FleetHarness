# Venue and IT

## Check-in

- [P] Go to one of the two ticket booths outside the building, left of the main entrance.
- [P] Bring valid government-issued ID. The name must match your portal registration.
- [P] Check in once. Wear the badge visibly and keep it for all days.
- [P] Nobody can collect a badge for someone else. Without the right ID or a matching registration, you cannot check in.

## Areas

- [P] NorVa Main Floor: kickoff, hacking and final judging.
- [P] NorVa Bar Area: boxed breakfast and lunch.
- [P] NorVa Outside Area: dinner.
- [P] IT Ops: 3rd floor.

## Getting there

- [P] The NorVa, 317 Monticello Avenue, Norfolk, VA 23510.
- [P] Parking: MacArthur Center, 300 Monticello Avenue, Norfolk, VA 23510.
- [P] Several hotels are within a 10-minute walk.

## What to bring

- [P] Government ID for check-in.
- [P] The devices you plan to use, government-furnished or personal, with chargers.
- [P] An Ethernet cable if you have one (messages suggest 8 feet, and the FAQ says CAT6), plus an adapter if needed.

## Food

- [P] Light breakfast and lunch are provided, with gluten-free, vegan and vegetarian options. Drinks are available all day.
- [P] Dinner is for purchase from on-site food trucks, and restaurants are nearby.

## Observers

- [P] Seating is reserved for active development teams, and observers have standing room only. If your registration role is wrong, email events@defenseunicorns.com.

## Wi-Fi

From the IT Rules of Engagement, event document 11 [P]:

| Network | Use |
| --- | --- |
| FLEETHACKER | The laptop and devices you need for hacking |
| FLEETHACKER-guest | Personal devices and personal use |

- Passwords are in document 11. They are not stored here.
- Do not run personal hotspots, access points, travel routers, wireless printers or other wireless network gear. Normal phone cellular service is fine, with the hotspot off.
- If you cannot connect, go to IT Ops rather than starting a hotspot.
- Wired access is limited. Bring a CAT6 patch cable and an adapter if you need one.

## Getting IT help

- [P] IT Ops on the 3rd floor, or the `#tech-support` channel.
- [P] Last resort if you cannot get online: stop at the desk or call or text (218) 206-2667.
- [P] Say where you are, your device and network, and what is happening. Do not include passwords or sensitive information.
- [P] IT Ops supports event networks and tools. Managed-device, account and VPN issues go to your own organization's IT team.
