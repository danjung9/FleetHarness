# AppYard: next 30, 60 and 90 days

[P] Rule 11 requires owners, a next experiment, resources and transition recommendations. [R] This is a proposed path, not an owner commitment or fielding authorization. Sources: [[Event/Rules]], [[Sprint/Submission-Checklist]].

| Responsibility | Owner and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Mission owner | [R] **[MUKUL TO SUPPLY: training-workflow sponsor and acceptance authority].** Confirm the task, current process and useful outcome. Source: [[Research/Plan/Plan]]. |
| Technical owner | [R] **[MUKUL TO SUPPLY: maintainer and release/support owner].** Own defects, dependencies, updates, rollback and shutdown. Source: [[Research/TaskApp/Pipeline-run1/path-to-fleet]]. |
| Security and data review | [R] **[MUKUL TO SUPPLY: responsible role and review route].** Approve any target device, data or integration beyond the synthetic demo. Source: [[Research/TaskApp/Pipeline-run1/path-to-fleet]]. |

[R] **Next experiment:** finish independent acceptance of one frozen artifact, then have a consenting relevant user perform the same synthetic task using their confirmed current method and the app. Record artifact identity, raw times, correctness, help and review/save success. Report the practice and setup limits; do not extrapolate Fleet savings. Sources: [[Research/QA/Acceptance/Officer-Walkthrough-11]], [[Research/QA/Officer/Trial-Protocol]], [[Research/QA/Officer/Burden-Claim]].

[R] **Resources:** permitted device and browser, synthetic examples, willing user, independent reviewer, maintainer time and security/support advice. Connected generation needs separately authorized model access and usage monitoring. **[MUKUL TO SUPPLY: availability, hours and budget].** Source: [[Research/Plan/Plan]].

| Gate | Recommendation and evidence to continue |
| --- | --- |
| 30 days | [R] Confirm owners and workflow. Close clock-display, review-binding, readable-export and guide gaps. Verify sources and durable action history. Complete the matched trial; assess correctness and total review effort before continuing. Sources: [[Research/QA/Acceptance/README]], [[Research/QA/Officer/Trial-Protocol]]. |
| 60 days | [R] With owner approval, pilot synthetic work on the intended government device/browser. Prove offline launch, recovery, installation, update and rollback. Resolve security/authorization requirements. If sync is required, demonstrate two-device conflict, interruption, retry and convergence. Sources: [[Research/RandE/Ideas/Scalability-Evidence]], [[Research/TaskApp/Pipeline-run1/path-to-fleet]]. |
| 90 days | [R] Owners decide transition, further testing or stop using measured usefulness, defects and support cost. Require an approved distribution route, license inventory, vulnerability response and support/rollback owners. Test a second domain only after first-task acceptance. Sources: [[Research/Plan/Plan]], [[Research/RandE/Ideas/Scalability-Evidence]]. |

[R] **Fleet conditions:** propose a reviewed local HTML artifact with local dependencies and saved drafts, distributed through an approved route. Test device restrictions, storage protection, limited connectivity and support. Retain a prior version for rollback. Sync remains absent; connected generation is separate from offline task use. Sources: [[Research/TaskApp/Pipeline-run1/path-to-fleet]], [[Research/RandE/Ideas/Scalability-Evidence]].

[R] **Human control:** the mission owner accepts scope; the technical owner monitors failures, changed versions and usage. Prohibit real records and operational writes without separate approval. On failed checks, prohibited data, lost drafts or failed shutdown, halt progression, preserve permitted evidence and escalate to those owners. Release requires a person's approval of freshly checked final bytes. Source: [[Research/Plan/Plan]].
