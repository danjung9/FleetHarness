## Problem

Public affairs planners use spreadsheets, email and unlinked trackers, so status chasing, slow advice to leaders, disconnected media products and manual after-action reports waste time.

## What to build

A first release for the NAVCO Navy Week planning desk with parent and child records, a request-to-AAR workflow, linked media tracking, an instant AAR summary, and a trade study of Power Apps versus a web front end.

## Requirements

- Parent-child relational SharePoint lists with required lookups
- Status flow from Requested to AAR Complete
- Human approval for funding, commitments and public releases
- Link queries, responses and earned media to events
- Compare Power Apps with SPFx/HTML5 by Day 2
- Unclassified only, IL5 boundaries
