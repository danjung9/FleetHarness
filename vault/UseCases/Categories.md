# Use case categories

Restated in our own words from the portal [P]. These six categories differ from the three challenge categories in [[Event/Rules]]; see [[Reference/Open-Questions]].

## Tactical Decision Advantage

Lower watchstander cognitive load during casualties, high-tempo evolutions, degraded sensors and shift changes, with source-cited and auditable decision support at the edge. Built on surrogate and public data.

## The Autonomous Shipboard Enterprise

Replace paper, re-keying, manual routing, tasking and records work with offline-first workflows that keep a human reviewer and sync when connectivity returns, within EMCON limits.

## Open Innovation

Edge SI prototypes outside the curated use cases, under tight power and bandwidth. Entries must meet the Fleet-Ready Definition of Done and use the same rubric.

## Fleet Readiness & Restore-to-Mission

Shorten the path from equipment casualty to restored mission capability by correlating procedures, maintenance history, parts, manning and schedule data, including on last-synced data. Sailors keep every decision.

## Cyber Resilience & Trusted Autonomy

Defend isolated, low-bandwidth networks and make agents safe to field: find and explain issues, then stage fixes only with human approval, audit and rollback. Its trusted-agent guardrails apply to SI solutions in every category.

## Path to Fleet

Take a working Sailor-built tool to a fieldable package (hosting, install path, security evidence, sustainment owner) and document the route for the next team. Any team may also fill in an optional Path to Fleet field.
