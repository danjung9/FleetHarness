## Problem

Many interacting AI-enabled systems become hard to predict, monitor and control even if each works alone.

## What to build

A multi-agent governance system that lets onboard AI share information, coordinate within authority limits and stay under human oversight.

## Requirements

- Information sharing between systems
- Coordinated actions
- Defined authority limits
- Human oversight
