## Problem

Leaders must decide with incomplete information and limited contact with higher headquarters, and a paper guide is the current aid.

## What to build

A prototype that follows Understand, Decide, Communicate, Adapt and covers the seven DSG elements, working offline with optional AI help.

## Requirements

- Cover Intent, Change, Information, Risk, Options, Ownership, Reassess
- Works without network, cloud or AI
- Structure human judgment, not replace it
- Usable under pressure
- Reinforce accountability and reassessment triggers
