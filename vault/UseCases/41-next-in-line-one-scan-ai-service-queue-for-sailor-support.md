## Problem

Sailors lose drill and duty time waiting in person; a Sailor-made QR queue at NRC Houston uses scattered commercial services, misroutes people and cannot scale.

## What to build

One Flank Speed queue with plain-language intake routing to the right department and document list, notifications, a live staff view, and easy configuration by other commands.

## Requirements

- Single QR entry for all departments
- AI intake routes and lists documents
- Virtual queue with turn notification
- Real-time staff view
- Within Power Apps, Power Automate, Teams, SharePoint/Dataverse
- Configurable without a developer
