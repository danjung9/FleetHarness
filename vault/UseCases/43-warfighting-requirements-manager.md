## Problem

Fleet warfighting requirements live in many workbooks, slide decks, PDFs and libraries, so version control, vetting and lifecycle auditing are poor.

## What to build

A portal to enter or bulk upload requirements, browse and search by status, approve through a PPBE workflow, number each one, log actions and export to Echelon I formats.

## Requirements

- Single or bulk entry
- Viewer with search, sort, filter, edit limited to approved users
- PPBE approve, reject or modify workflow
- Serialized tracking numbers
- Export to Excel, PowerPoint, Word, PDF
- Action logging

- NIST
