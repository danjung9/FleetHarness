## Problem

Navy commands build digital products in silos, so people cannot find existing work or the right experts, causing duplicated effort.

## What to build

A prototype discovery hub with catalog entries, keyword and natural-language search, ranked results and a Related Projects feature.

## Requirements

- Catalog fields: name, description, type, domain, owner, status, POC, links, tags, access
- Keyword and similarity search
- Ranked natural-language results
- Related Projects ranking
- Respect access and release policies
