## Problem

Dispersed acquisition teams hold data in disconnected legacy systems, and enterprise AI tools lack live connectors, forcing manual uploads in isolated chats.

## What to build

An interim shared AI notebook loaded with standardized exports so remote teams can jointly query one source-grounded picture, with humans making final decisions.

## Requirements

- Simultaneous secure shared access
- Ingest PDF, TXT, CSV and Google Docs/Slides
- Answers strictly grounded with citations to pages or rows
- Enterprise, division and program views
- Humans keep all approvals
