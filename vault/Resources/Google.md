# Google

Sources: event documents 4 and 7, in our own words [P].

## Access

Each team uses a unique link to reach a Google workspace and a browser-based VM with Antigravity already installed.

1. Get the team link from your team lead or from Events. One environment supports 6 to 7 people.
2. Open it, confirm the team or table number, enter your email and choose to access your credentials.
3. Follow the instructions there to reach the team's Google Cloud console and its preconfigured VM.
4. In the lab, expand the Antigravity URL and open the remote desktop link in your browser.
5. Follow the sign-in instructions for Antigravity, Gemini Enterprise and the other tools.

No team or link yet: events@defenseunicorns.com. Keep supplied credentials inside your team.

## Which tool for what

| Tool | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Gemini Notebook | Research team references and prepare presentations |
| Gemini Enterprise, Agent Builder | No-code or low-code agents and workflows |
| Antigravity | Build and test custom applications |

Suggested flow: research the problem, build the solution, then prepare the pitch.

## Training

- Google Skills: no-cost, on-demand courses for military personnel. An account is required and is separate from the event environment.
- Accelerating Efficiency with SI Agents: beginner, 90 minutes, virtual.
- Build Agent Skills with Google: intermediate, 2 hours, on demand.
- Build Agents with CX Agent Studio: advanced, on demand, 5 modules plus 2 labs.
- Short videos: Agent Builder, Antigravity for beginners, Antigravity 2.0 guides. One video covers running local Gemma models offline, which may matter for DDIL.
