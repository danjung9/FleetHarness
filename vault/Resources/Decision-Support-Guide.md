# Decision Support Guide v4.0

Source: event document 13, one page, in our own words [P]. Its subtitle matches [[UseCases/48-decision-autonomy-under-uncertainty]], so the two are probably related [A].

It is a lightweight aid for turning commander's intent into disciplined autonomous action, meant to complement existing planning rather than replace it. Its flow is understand, decide, communicate, adapt.

## Seven prompts

| Step | Question it asks |
| --- | --- |
| Intent | What must be achieved or preserved, and what cannot fail? |
| Change | What changed, and how does it affect the intent? |
| Information | What is known, assumed and unknown, and which unknown could change the decision? |
| Risk | What is at risk to mission and force, and what loss is acceptable? |
| Options and trade-offs | What are the feasible options, and what does each gain and cost? |
| Ownership | Am I authorized to accept this risk, who bears the effects, and what if higher authority is unreachable? |
| Reassess | What concrete trigger would change the decision? |

## Decision-ready brief

Fields: recommend, intent, risk, trade-off, ownership, trigger.

A closing final check tests the recommendation against intent and risk ownership. Its last lines were cut off in our capture.
