# SpaceXSI

Source: event document 9, in our own words [P]. The portal's sponsor list names this sponsor xAI.

## Steps

1. Use the redemption link or QR code in document 9.
2. Sign in or create the account the redemption page asks for.
3. Redeem the offer and confirm it applied.
4. Open Cursor and sign in with that same account.

The document also links downloads for Cursor and Grok Bot.

From event messages, in our own words [P]:

- Grok Bot: handles work around the code, such as research, documentation and briefings.

The link and QR code are for registered participants only and must not be shared outside the event, so they are not stored here.
