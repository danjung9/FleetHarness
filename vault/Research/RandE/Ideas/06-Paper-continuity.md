# Keep checking when the device is gone

## Bottom line

[R] Export an unfinished draft as a paper worksheet containing its inputs and a short manual checking grid. [R] Include room overlaps, person overlaps, rest and minutes totals with the exact fictional rules and draft revision. [A] A second person could continue this tiny task without the original device.

- **Moment [R]:** Close the laptop and hand over the four-request worksheet. The next reviewer finds one planted conflict and marks the correction as an unverified paper draft.
- **Novel increment [E/A]:** Printable summaries, offline apps and maintainer handoff already exist in the recommendations. This proposes a human-executable continuation procedure for the training checker, beyond a readable evidence page.
- **Judge evidence [R]:** Check whether another person finishes the same task correctly without coaching. Paper marks confer no software-tested status; later re-entry requires full checks and review.
- **Size [A]:** 2 to 4 person-hours for a fixed worksheet and handover trial, excluding general paper import.
- **Verdict [R]:** Kill for Thursday. It duplicates logic, can go stale, and could add transcription work. Reconsider only if device loss is a confirmed problem and a paper method beats the existing manual process.
