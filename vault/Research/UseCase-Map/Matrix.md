# Use case feasibility and judging matrix

## Bottom line

[E] This table covers the 34 IDs in the captured `use_cases` array, with one row per ID. [A] Difficulty, data sufficiency, disconnected feasibility and scores describe a bounded prototype by the November 5 submission deadline, not a fielded system. [R] Prefer a narrow result with an observable before-and-after task over a broad interface with unverified integrations. [A] The five judging estimates are provisional engineering judgments with approximately 15-point uncertainty in either direction.

## How to read the table

[P] Judging weights are impact 30%, Speed to Fleet 20%, scalability 20%, barrier removal 15% and judges' discretion 15%, each on a 0 to 100 scale, per [[Event/Judging]]. [A] The five score columns estimate what a credible bounded demonstration might support if it works; no judges, users or measured trials supplied these numbers. [A] Speed scores also discount deployment and authorization obstacles; synthetic success alone does not establish fielding speed. [R] Do not treat small score differences as a ranking or a promise of award results.

[A] L means low, M medium, H high, VH very high implementation difficulty relative to the deadline. [A] M-H means the scope or assets determine the difficulty. [A] These are independent build estimates, not the portal difficulty field, which is absent for many records. [P] Team counts are `teams_using_count` from the capture, not current competition or evidence that a team has implemented anything.

[A] A useful disconnected version means completing the named local task with preloaded inputs and all needed software installed. [A] It does not mean fresh remote data, remote approvals, WAN notifications or successful shore delivery during outage. [A] Public/synthetic sufficiency means enough to demonstrate the stated slice, never enough by itself to validate mission accuracy, safety, authorization or an enterprise connector. [R] Any actual government data, including unclassified data, must pass rule 4; no real personal data belongs in these examples.

[R] Family definitions and reusable tests are in [[Research/UseCase-Map/Archetypes]]. [A] Primary users are inferred workflow roles, not confirmed interview participants. [P] Data categories summarize the submissions; [A] their minimum fixture contents and availability are design assumptions.
