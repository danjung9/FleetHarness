# Training draft burden claim

## Bottom line

[R] Name the proposed user as a training coordinator preparing a draft for a reviewing officer, without collecting a person's name. [A] Manually reconciling proposed training times against supplied people, rooms and qualifications is our workflow hypothesis until a relevant user confirms its general fit. [V] Published Navy and GAO material supports investigating training coordination and workload, but supplies no baseline for this four-request task. [R] With no trial, report no measured reduction; with one trial, report only the observed task result, its quality and its limits.

## Claim with no trial

[R] Use this wording, retaining its uncertainty:

> Our proposed user is a training coordinator preparing a draft for a reviewing officer. Public Navy reporting describes local training scheduling, and GAO has documented training workload and fragmented maintenance information systems. Our four-request fictional exercise tests checking supplied constraints and retaining a human-reviewed draft. We have not measured this user's current burden or a reduction in time, errors or cognitive effort. The current workflow and usefulness of this task still need user confirmation.

[V] The contextual statements above are supported by sources 1 to 3 below. [A] The proposed user, manual reconciliation workflow and expected benefit are hypotheses. Four requests is the size of a synthetic test, not an impact number. [R] Do not substitute a build speed, test pass count, public fatigue statistic, promotional savings percentage or unobserved demonstration for user benefit. An unrun protocol is not validation, and absence of a measured saving does not mean measured savings of zero.

[R] For the submission's current-process field use: “Proposed process, not yet confirmed: manually check proposed events, resolve supplied conflicts, review and retain a draft. Current burden: not measured.” The role is specific; the participant's identity is unnecessary. This does not establish organizer acceptance of the evidence or satisfaction of Rule 2.

## Claim after one trial

[R] Use [[Research/QA/Officer/Trial-Protocol]]. Retain the anonymous results sheet, task input, both attempts, exact app identity and timing method. Populate this template only from an actual record; all blanks below are deliberately unfilled. Use “participant” if the person did not volunteer a role. Do not call them a training coordinator unless volunteered role and task familiarity support it.

> On [date], one consenting Sailor or officer completed the same four-request synthetic training task first using [paper/spreadsheet] and then [artifact identity, solver/checker]. Both attempts met the predefined correctness, review and retained-draft criteria. Stopwatch times were [B] seconds and [A] seconds, a difference of [B minus A] seconds. Correct rows were [b]/4 and [a]/4; failed rule types were [b]/8 and [a]/8; help interventions were [b] and [a]. Ease ratings were [b]/7 and [a]/7. The participant [confirmed/did not confirm] that this comparison resembled their general process. This is one person's repeated synthetic task with prefilled inputs, the app second, and setup excluded. It is not an estimate of Fleet-wide savings or proof the app caused the difference.

[R] Completed observations receive `[E, anonymous trial record, date and artifact]`, not `[V]`: the future trial is primary local evidence, not a published study. Public sources remain context only. The template currently makes no claim that a person was recruited, consented, completed a task or endorsed the app.

[R] When both attempts succeed, calculate `difference_seconds = baseline_seconds - app_seconds`. A positive difference means the second attempt was faster; a negative difference means it was slower. Optional `100 * difference_seconds / baseline_seconds` is a descriptive percentage for this pair only, with a nonzero baseline. Prefer seconds to a headline percentage. Preserve slower, unchanged and unsuccessful outcomes.

[R] Include participant review, discovery, edits, assistance and waiting in elapsed time. Separately report setup, wait and help. Do not call elapsed seconds labor saved or net person-minutes saved: facilitator and independent reviewer effort, real data entry, operational approval, recurring maintenance and training costs are not fully measured by this ten-minute exercise. A reported ease rating is subjective, not validated cognitive-load reduction.

## If the comparison cannot support a savings number

| Observed situation [R] | Permitted wording |
| --- | --- |
| Baseline times out; app succeeds | “The baseline was unfinished at the 180-second cap; the app attempt finished correctly in [A] seconds.” Do not treat 180 as a completion time or calculate a saving. |
| Either attempt has an incorrect result, needs a supplied answer, or lacks the retained reviewed draft | Report times as time spent to that outcome, with error/help counts. Do not compare them as time to equivalent success. |
| Participant recalls a usual duration; it was not timed | “Participant-reported **ESTIMATE**: [range and unit] for [general task]. Not observed.” Never subtract an app time and label the result measured savings. |
| Paper/spreadsheet is not representative, or participant does not do comparable work | “One synthetic usability observation; current-workflow burden remains unverified.” Do not claim validated user demand. |
| No consent, missing accepted app, stopped trial or missing timer record | No paired measured-burden claim. State what was not run or not measured, without retaining withdrawn participant data. |

[R] App-second practice, task familiarity, a four-request ceiling, prefilled inputs and a single convenience participant prevent causal attribution or representative estimates. Do not multiply a one-person difference by ships, personnel or assumed task frequency. If someone requests an extrapolation, label every frequency, generalization and derived result **[A, ESTIMATE]**, show assumptions and excluded costs, and keep it out of the measured-impact headline. There is no defensible annual savings estimate in this record.

[R] A matched observation can strengthen the problem statement; it does not by itself establish real operational burden, full ID 11 coverage, field readiness, fatigue-policy compliance, synchronization, offline behavior or official approval. The app produces a synthetic draft. A person retains review and approval authority.
