# Ten-minute training draft trial

## Bottom line

[R] Run Thursday morning, November 5, with one willing Sailor or officer, preferably familiar with training drafts. The target user is a training coordinator, not a named person. App-second practice limits any benefit claim.

**Prepare beforehand [R]:** Accepted task app/checker with identity/receipt, stopwatch, card, paper or local spreadsheet. Prefill identical inputs, with B proposed at 0800; leave results blank. Hide the key and, during the app attempt, the baseline answer. Record setup seconds separately. No accepted artifact: record NOT RUN; never substitute the factory/hosted preview.

**Synthetic card [R]:** All requests last 60 minutes. People/operations are available 0800 to 1200 both days. P01 holds Q-A; P02 holds Q-B. Each has a 120-minute maximum across BOTH days. P01 needs 30 minutes between sessions; P02 needs zero. Everything is invented. Keep all requests, people, rooms and durations; avoid overlaps and use permitted starts.

**Run and say exactly [R], elapsed session minutes:**

- **0:00 to 1:00:** “Will you try a ten-minute fictional task? Stop anytime. May we record anonymous results for event evidence? No names, units, real records, photos or recordings. Do you prepare training drafts? Is paper or spreadsheet a fair comparison?” Declined consent: end, retain nothing.
- **1:00 to 4:00, baseline:** Reveal card at Go. “Check these requests, correct conflicts, show start/end times and each person's total minutes. Explain changes. Check your answer, mark it reviewed as a draft, then hand over the paper or save the sheet. Say done. Go.” Stop at done or 180 seconds. Freeze output; no answer feedback.
- **4:00 to 4:30:** “How easy was that, from 1, very difficult, to 7, very easy?” Reset app to identical inputs, with no solved result visible.
- **4:30 to 7:30, app:** Repeat baseline instruction, replacing handover with “save the draft locally.” Time Go to done or 180 seconds, including discovery, checks, review, corrections, help, wait and save. No coaching; if asked, say “Use the card and tell me when finished.” Count all help.
- **7:30 to 9:00:** “How easy was that, 1 to 7? Why change B? Who approves real use? What was hardest? Is this fair to your usual general process?” Summarize anonymously. Interrupt sensitive details: “Please keep to this fictional example.”
- **9:00 to 10:00:** Score frozen attempts; confirm anonymous summary. No scored retries. Withdrawal, prohibited data or unexpected external action: stop, close app, escalate to Mukul without sensitive details; discard withdrawn results.

**Score [R], hidden key:** A/C day 1 0800 to 0900; B 0930 to 1030; D day 2 0800 to 0900; totals 120 each. B resolves person/room overlap, preserving 30 minutes rest. Count exact rows /4, distinct failed rule types /8 (starts, duration, qualifications, windows, person/room overlaps separately, rest, minutes limit), row-edit events and help interventions. Success requires all rows once, zero failures, correct totals/explanation, review and retained draft. Timeout means unfinished at 180 seconds. Unknown is blank, not zero.
