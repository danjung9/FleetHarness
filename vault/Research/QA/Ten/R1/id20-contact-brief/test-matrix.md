## Risks and review boundaries

Eligibility metadata simulates a retrieval boundary and is not authentication or access control. Public registry snapshots and representative ambiguous cases are missing; the planner-defined synthetic twins are engineering fixtures, not authoritative vessel examples. Lexical coverage is not confidence, identity proof or applicability. A citation supports only its configured field, never inferred intent or sanctions clearance. Stale, missing, future and conflicting evidence must remain visible uncertainty. Stable local snapshot IDs and editable inline text do not establish immutable storage.

Human review must assess readability, keyboard focus, narrow layout, one-page print fit, usefulness, source applicability and the independent expected outcomes. The host browser journeys establish only their specified interactions; no browser/visual inspection has occurred during planning. Operational review still needs approved data rights, authorized connectors, trusted access controls, integrity/retention design, domain freshness/identity policies and owner evaluation. No local result establishes a government connection, authority to operate, security certification or operational intelligence validity.
