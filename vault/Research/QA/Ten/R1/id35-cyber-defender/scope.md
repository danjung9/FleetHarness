`challenge` describes finding, explaining and fixing misconfigurations in an isolated shipboard-like enclave after a new STIG release and critical vulnerability bulletin, with human approval, verification, audit and rollback. The intended fleet benefit is faster verified remediation with less manual effort. `userScope` repeats that broad scenario and its six evaluation criteria; it supplies no narrower operational permission. The prototype slice below is an explicit engineering scope reduction required by the synthetic-only boundary and missing lab, not a change to the source requirements.

## Audience, task and observable outcome

Primary audience: cyber/IT analysts investigating an offline evidence bundle. Reviewers are system owners or authorized change approvers; commanders and data owners receive the risk explanation. A locally entered reviewer label represents a synthetic role, not authenticated authority.

Smallest complete prototype task: review synthetic offline asset/configuration/log evidence against a versioned synthetic control/bulletin example; distinguish one seeded issue from a benign look-alike; prepare an evidence-linked finding and a specific change proposal; record a human review decision for its exact revision; export an analyst handoff containing risk, review status, audit history, verification steps and rollback steps. A changed proposal requires fresh review. This is an evidence-and-proposal workflow rather than a general chat interface.

Observable success: an analyst can produce a portable handoff in which the seeded issue and benign case match the independent synthetic answer key, each finding traces to asset and control evidence, the proposed change has an identifiable revision and review state, and all execution/verification/rollback statuses remain explicitly **not executed / not verified**. A stale review cannot appear current after an edit. No result is reported as a remediated asset.

## Inputs and output information contract

These are minimum information needs, not a chosen schema or implementation design. All identifiers and contents must be synthetic, with unambiguous fixture provenance.

Export must retain citations, review invalidation, rollback/verification instructions and the unexecuted status. Exact serialization and persistence choices remain for later phases.

## Operating environment, recovery and safety boundary

Intended prototype environment: a local browser with bundled synthetic reference examples and local evidence input, suitable for a disconnected demonstration. Browser support, storage/retention, bundle limits and reconnect behavior are not yet specified. No runtime cloud, scanning or operational service is required by the scoped task. Actual offline behavior and desktop/narrow-browser outcomes require later application checks.

Recovery needs: malformed input, missing asset/control references, unsupported example versions and insufficient evidence receive specific errors without losing an analyst draft; no partial import may silently produce confirmed findings. The analyst can correct and retry. Rejected reviews can be revised and resubmitted; edits invalidate earlier review. Export failure preserves the proposal for retry. Local data loss/reset and retention behavior need an explicit later decision and user-visible disclosure. Unknown applicability must be visible instead of guessed.

Data policy is **synthetic-only**. Do not fetch operational records, real configurations/logs, credentials, identities or protected data. Sanitized snapshots mentioned by the challenge are missing integration needs, not permission to ingest real records. Source resources list a cyber range, public DISA/NVD/CVE feeds, sample configurations/logs, link emulation and SMEs; none is established as available by the packet. No live feeds or external service access is included in this scope.

Explicit exclusions: live scanning, patching, exploitation, network discovery, identity administration, CAC integration, live Navy networks, production synchronization, deployment and operational changes. No execution adapter or isolated training range is supplied. Cyber work stops at evidence and planning until a lab adapter and reviewed tests exist. Review-state demonstrations, toolkit calculations and reference fixtures do not establish application verification, operational remediation, clean rollback, STIG compliance or fleet readiness.

## Measurable outcome boundaries

For the proposed synthetic slice, assess finding correctness and false positives against an independent key, completeness of required evidence references and handoff fields, review-to-revision consistency, and the presence of expected audit events. Do not use toolkit self-tests or calculated fixture answers as application verification. No measurements have been run at intake. Application journeys, literal expected results and recovery checks belong to planning and verification, as specified by the quality contract.

Source performance measures remain detection share, time to a correct plan, approved application with rollback, emulated-link operation and complete audit coverage. Benefit measures require elapsed time to independently verified remediation and manual analyst hours against a comparable baseline. The owner has provided no numerical performance threshold, baseline or link budget; intake does not invent one.
