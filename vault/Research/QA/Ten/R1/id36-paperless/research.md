# Paperless Underway, reuse research

Research date: 2026-11-04. Phase: research only. Data boundary: synthetic-only.

The research recommendation is to **adapt IndexedDB** for the scoped browser-persistence use case, **adapt the supplied immutable-revision primitive** for simulated reconciliation, and retain **PouchDB/CouchDB as a conditional adapt candidate for a future authorized deployment**. These are fit assessments, not a selected architecture, installation approval, implementation plan or verified compatibility finding. No dependency, connector, browser workflow or operational integration was exercised.

## Concrete risks and open questions for planning

- **Durability and offline boot:** Which devices, browser versions, quota/eviction controls and offline installation mechanism apply? Transaction success, application availability offline, reload persistence and real device restart resilience are separate evidence needs. Failed saves must preserve the draft and avoid a saved status.
- **Conflict and audit integrity:** Who can review and resolve each head, including delete versus edit? What invalidates review? Preserve both branches and the resolution decision; duplicate operation replay must not create duplicate record effects. Identity, tamper resistance, audit retention and deletion retention are unspecified.
- **Replication choice:** The local primitive is the concrete available option for the labeled simulator. PouchDB/CouchDB is the established-replication candidate for comparison before any real protocol extension. Missing release/license and semantic evidence prevents an unconditional adoption recommendation. Determine adapter support, pruning effects, access control, offline authorization expiry and owner-platform constraints before operational use.
- **Transfer and routing meaning:** Is offline routing a pending local package or an authorized disconnected-network transfer? What is an authoritative receipt? No receiver exists here. OFF-04 requires the interrupted-photo scenario, bounded bytes, critical-record priority and acknowledged resume offsets; record reconciliation cannot substitute for it.
- **Domain inputs:** No official blank forms, current correspondence-rule text, records schedule, scans, independent answer key or SME baseline is supplied. The training-log schema remains provisional. Extraction accuracy (EVAL36-1), time savings (EVAL36-3 / DELIVERABLE36-1), reduced rework (DELIVERABLE36-2), panel usability (EVAL36-5) and official scoring (EVAL36-6) cannot be established by reuse choices.
- **Security and platform integration:** UC36-4 and broad fleet readiness need authorized government-device/low-code evidence. Device encryption requires a validated device/key design; no browser API, fixture actor or project guide establishes it. Retention, backup, device loss, authentication and actual transport remain unresolved.

These are research options and evidence gaps for the planner, not a task sequence or acceptance disposition. All 20 canonical IDs and USER36 provenance aliases remain as recorded in the packet and scope. Planning owns literal outcomes and deferrals under the quality contract; partial demonstrations cannot satisfy broad requirements. Later actual application tests must cover replay, opposite merge orders, concurrent edit/delete, missing history and queue preservation; browser evidence must separately cover persistence and the offline-to-reconnect workflow. Toolkit execution or lexical retrieval cannot replace those gates.
