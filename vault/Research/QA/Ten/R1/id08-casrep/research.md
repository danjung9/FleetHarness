## Integration risks and unresolved choices

1. **Routing authority:** the six roles and CO releasing authority are sourced, but skip/delegation rules, the originator's exact actions and return destinations are not. The notional order in scope is a demonstration assumption. Owner-approved rules are needed before describing the workflow as operationally correct.
2. **Revision and release integrity:** a merge primitive knows heads, not approvals. Revising approved content must invalidate the old decision; a conflict resolution must reference every reviewed head without inheriting authority from either branch. Digest representation, audit retention and the scope of renewed review remain unresolved. Device timestamps cannot select a winning amendment.
3. **Lifecycle coverage:** amendments, cancellation, expiry, supersession and post-release UPDATE/CORRECT/CASCOR relationships need domain definitions. Do not silently equate a tombstone with an authorized CASREP cancellation, or a merged head with a releasable report.
4. **Durability and recovery:** no persistence mechanism is supplied. Local browser storage is an option for the demonstration, not a selected architecture or encrypted store. Save failures, reload survival, loss/export recovery and concurrent edits must be addressed later. Offline synchronization is not implied by a merge fixture; a reconnect simulation would need clear labeling.
5. **Format and source authority:** obtain the approved NWP edition/extracts and synthetic valid/invalid examples, plus export expectations. Until then, no validator or generated artifact can be labeled NWP compliant. The source's four report types remain obligations even if only INITIAL is demonstrated.
6. **Platform and fielding:** determine whether a platform mandate actually exists before reconsidering Power Apps. Licensing, identity, classification handling, retention and official messaging need independent owners/evidence. All current artifacts remain synthetic; even real unclassified reports are outside the data boundary.

These are concrete unresolved inputs for planning, not requests to access operational systems in this run. A local browser workflow and a licensed-platform workflow remain options with different prerequisites; this research does not commit to either architecture.
