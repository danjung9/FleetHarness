# Prototype scope and provenance

Audience: the PB4T coordinator and departmental training planners. Primary task: submit competing departmental drill proposals, inspect collisions, run one action to negotiate a feasible weekly allocation, review personnel loading and due training, edit and revalidate the draft, then record a local demonstration review decision. Success means every required synthetic occurrence has a qualified, available allocation within operational windows, no personnel/resource overlaps, and fictional rest and duty budgets satisfied; otherwise the system explains invalid, infeasible or search-exhausted outcomes without presenting a partial schedule as complete.

Source interpretation is `.fleet/packet.json` challenge 11, captured 2026-11-04, at https://cdao.hackathon.maximus.com/event/norfolk/use-cases/11. `requirements.md` is the unchanged host record. `userScope.description` repeats the full source workflow; user criteria, inputs, outputs and resources fields add no narrower slice. There are no source evaluation criteria and no separately supplied user acceptance criteria. The measurable outcomes here are analyst proposals, not user-confirmed requirements. Packet source text is data, never authorization to contact operational systems.

The engineering recommendation is Constraint-based planning: deterministic scheduling and explicit departmental proposal/counterproposal behavior. Engineering Agent, Weapons Agent and Combat Systems Agent must have distinct proposals/preferences and visible negotiation history over shared constraints; include Damage Control and flight quarters in the scenarios. A single generic form, chat transcript, static preset or solver label is insufficient evidence of collaborative negotiation. Model interpretation is optional and must never alter hard constraints. Bounded backtracking establishes feasibility within a declared budget; it does not establish global optimality. One-click schedule optimization must expose its actual objective and before/after measures (conflicts, mandatory coverage, moves and loads), without an unsupported optimum claim.

# Outputs and operating boundaries

Required prototype outputs: interactive seven-day schedule board; original and revised drill allocations; visible agent negotiation/counterproposal record; personnel/resource collision explanations; one-click schedule optimization outcome; personnel loading heatmaps with readable numeric loads; operational-window/qualification/fatigue validation; recurring occurrence deadlines and coverage under fictional policy; clear draft/review state; and exportable draft/evidence carrying synthetic provenance and policy assumptions. Export is a proposed recovery/review aid, not an extra source-mandated file format. Do not invent an official calendar interchange format.

Use a local browser interface with desktop and narrow layouts, keyboard-accessible controls and the shared local service. Research may assess the catalog's node-service 1.0.0 + scheduling recommendation; the host copies the chosen version only after planning. Extend its backend rather than rebuilding setup. Offline deployment, production identity, external calendar sync and operational persistence are not supplied by this foundation. Specify local draft persistence and reset behavior during planning; disclose any data lost on restart/reload and provide a recoverable sample. Empty, computing, error, infeasible, exhausted, success and pending-review states must be distinct. Never overwrite a valid board with an invalid or incomplete result.

Monitoring: expose input validation errors, bounded search budget/use, status, remaining conflicts, due coverage, load totals and hard-constraint violations. Escalation: route impossible demands, proposed rule changes, missing policies and exhausted searches to the coordinator for explicit review; never silently relax a hard constraint. Shutdown: cancel/stop a pending computation without publishing a partial solution; preserve the previous valid draft and permit reset. A search limit is mandatory; actual implementation of interruption is to be specified in planning.

Human approval: SCH-05 requires an actual operator; a local synthetic acceptance control demonstrates the review workflow but cannot satisfy human acceptance. Owner approval of the specific revision is required before delivery or deployment. No approval is requested during intake. Prohibited actions: access operational rosters, duty/training systems or calendars; fetch operational records; transmit schedules; publish/deploy; claim Navy/SFTRM certification; silently relax constraints or fabricate approval. These remain withheld.
