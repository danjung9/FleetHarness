Input grammar & mapping

Three headers: SYN-OPTASK/1//, MSGID/name//, NOTICE/SYNTHETIC-NOT-REAL-USMTF//. FREQ → frequency plans; CRYPTO → crypto change-over windows; FLIGHT → flight quarters points. RMKS is inert. This invented mapping needs real-format validation.

Use the sample field order, explicit YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:00Z or numeric offsets. Correct uncertain fields in the raw draft and parse again. No implicit date, timezone, duration or overnight rollover.
