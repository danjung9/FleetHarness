## Concrete integration risks and planning handoff

- **Fixture boundary:** INITIAL content/sourceMessage describes revision A, while final state/rendered output describes released B. Importing the final state as a new draft would skip the return/revision workflow. There are exactly two regular INITIAL revisions, 12 successful events and five reviewer approvals; comment, return, submission and release are distinct actions. Expected fixture answers must not be trusted as computational inputs or packaged as hidden runtime answers. Embed only needed sample data in the self-contained served HTML, preserving the validated files.
- **Protocol mismatch:** The reconciler accepts string values up to 50,000 characters or null, at most 5,000 revisions per input side and 100 parent IDs. Domain snapshots have additional fields. Canonical serialization, domain metadata retention, field-level comparison and error vocabulary need explicit consideration. A conflict resolution must reference both reviewed heads, preserve all five fixture revisions and invalidate earlier approvals; wall-clock order cannot choose a winner.
- **Authority and lifecycle:** Backend checks must bind actions to actor, stage and current revision, even if UI buttons are hidden. Demo actor selection is not authentication. CO approval and mock release are separate; originator return and CO forward are invalid in supplied rules. INITIAL → UPDATE → CORRECT → CASCOR are related reports with fresh routes, not four revisions of one report. Parser-only examples must remain distinct from runtime predecessor-release requirements. Cancellation, expiry and supersession semantics mentioned by workflow guidance have no validated Navy policy or fixture answer here; preserve their authority/lifecycle boundary without inventing operational rules.
- **Durability and recovery:** Neither foundation nor reconciler supplies durable offline storage. Local persistence must preserve the last committed state through reload/restart, stale concurrent edits and save/export failures. Mutable service data cannot overwrite declared deployment source files. A save acknowledgement must follow persistence; a failed export must not produce a partial success download. Any reconnect simulator must be labeled and cannot establish real transport or encryption.
- **Exact outputs and safety:** Preserve fixed Unicode, LF, insertion order and terminal newline for exact fixture exportText checks. Keep instruction-like narrative inert through parser, preview and export. Reject impossible dates rather than normalizing them. Separate rejected-attempt monitoring from successful audit events; its timestamps/serialization remain deliberately open to planning. No operational overdue thresholds are supplied.
- **Runtime reuse:** The host copies the selected foundation only after planning. Inspect its actual shared computation, error behavior and lock provenance then. Browser and host invocation must use that same backend. No new framework, AI service or database dependency is justified by this research; persistence choice remains a planning decision and must satisfy restart/concurrency cases.
- **Operational integrations:** No identity provider, CAC role mapping, Microsoft tenant, official release API, classified system, retention policy or maintenance connector is configured. The eventual one-page brief must distinguish proposed integration from exercised capability. Independent authorization and domain review are needed before any operational use; synthetic release is local only.
