# CASREP Command fielding brief
Synthetic, not validated against the real CASREP format.

The local prototype provides immutable message drafting, six-role routing, return/revision, comments, revision-bound approvals, explicit simulated CO release and a message/audit export. All identities, units, equipment and rule versions are fictional. The role selector demonstrates permissions; it does not authenticate users.

The originator saves and submits. Division Officer, Department Head, Engineer Officer/CHENG and XO approve/forward or return the current revision. CO separately approves and mock-releases. Edits invalidate approvals. Released snapshots remain immutable; UPDATE, CORRECT and CASCOR start linked records without inherited reviews. Cancellation, expiry and supersession are local notices only.

Only synthetic information is permitted. The marking validator is a demonstration, not a classification detector. Do not enter real or classified information. Plain filesystem and browser storage are not encrypted. A classified deployment requires independently approved device, hosting, identity, network separation, keys, retention, backup and incident controls.

No Navy messaging, Microsoft 365 or identity connector is configured. An operational adapter must use approved message rules, authenticated authority, CO release authorization and receiver acknowledgements, preserve version digests and reject stale approvals. Local exports never establish official release.

Sponsor and sustainment owner are unresolved. Before fielding, an owner must validate NWP 1-03.1 and OPNAVINST 4790.4 mapping, route/delegation policy, amendment/cancellation semantics, classification handling, retention and access enforcement; exercise concurrent users and failure recovery on approved devices; and authorize hosting and release-channel integration. Success means reviewed message-format equivalence, no unauthorized release, recoverable durable edits and acknowledged authorized transmission. No readiness or time benefit has been measured. Owner approval is required before delivery or deployment.
