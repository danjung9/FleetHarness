## Audience, task and success

Expeditionary-unit tablet users capture forms while disconnected; command staff consolidate a week of tasking; a reviewer approves corrected packets and explicit conflict resolutions before routing. The smallest complete demonstration is one synthetic packet containing all three Track A document types plus a linked week of Track B emails/documents. A generic record editor or preset action list is insufficient.

Observable prototype success: source-backed extracted fields and tasks are reviewed, invalid items are blocked, corrections persist across reload offline, routing is queued locally with a named synthetic destination, reconnecting to a local peer simulation reconciles revisions without loss or duplicate effect, and the reviewed packet, verified action list, draft status updates, filing decisions and audit evidence can be inspected/exported. Offline routing means durable local dispatch/receipt within the simulated workflow, never a claim of receipt by an unreachable remote command. Distinguish queued, transferred, acknowledged and filed states.

## Complete workflow and authority

1. Open embedded synthetic samples offline, or capture a supported synthetic handwritten scan and textual document. Retain the original artifact and source coordinates/line references. Extract training log fields, supply requisition fields and correspondence metadata/content; do not merely display a supplied answer key.
2. Extract a week of tasking emails and documents into proposed actions with owner role, due time, source, status and dependencies. Recognize duplicate tasking, amendments and ambiguous requests. Generate draft status updates and records filing recommendations from reviewed actions and a labeled synthetic records schedule.
3. Validate required fields, dates, quantities, correspondence structure and record categories using documented synthetic rules. Flag uncertain handwriting and ambiguous assignments for human correction; never invent missing values. Review every extracted item and any draft before submission.
4. Save locally before acknowledging success; retain immutable operation IDs, parents, reviewer decisions, sources and audit entries. Two fictional devices edit shared records; preserve concurrent edit/delete heads, prohibit silent last-writer loss, and require explicit human merge. Editing approved content invalidates approval.
5. Queue only approved versions. Under a bounded synthetic link budget, send critical record metadata first and resume attachments from acknowledged offsets after interruption. Deduplicate replay and converge regardless of delivery order. Approval is a local role simulation, not authenticated identity.
6. Export the completed forms, reviewed routing package, verified action list, draft status updates, records filing manifest, source references, audit trail and a one-page path to fleet. No government endpoint, actual tasking email, official records repository or external recipient is contacted.
