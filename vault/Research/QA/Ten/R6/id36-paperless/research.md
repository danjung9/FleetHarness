## Concrete mismatches and integration risks for planning

1. **Priority/chunk semantics differ.** Fixture priority 0 metadata precedes priority 1 photo, then attachment ID; maximum chunk is 128 bytes. Inspected planUploads instead sorts larger priority first, then age and ID, and emits one transfer range per item without chunking. Adapt semantics explicitly in the shared application core. Preserve supplied fixtures; do not invert their answer key to match the toolkit. Budget 400 expects metadata [0,64), then photo [256,384), [384,512), [512,592). Two acknowledgements persist only metadata=64 and photo=384. A planned/sent chunk is not acknowledged progress; zero budget retains pending work. Planned pendingIds is not a durable queue.
2. **Revision schema differs.** Fixture revisionId/operationId/parentIds/replicaId/payload/tombstone must map without losing identity, provenance or approval data to toolkit id/parents/actor/string value or null. R1/R2 and R1/RD are alternative scenarios, not one imported graph. RM chooses 7 explicitly, while base REC-SUP quantity remains 4. Missing history must preserve queue and request ancestry. Wall clocks never choose winners. Review and routing bind exact revisions; editing invalidates approval.
3. **Offline availability is not supplied by HTTP.** Local service with internet disconnected can execute locally if available on the device. A browser on another device loses compute when that service is unreachable. Planning must state and prove the actual supported topology. Preserve one shared computational implementation for UI and invocation; do not maintain inconsistent browser/backend rules. Self-contained samples and local storage alone do not establish complete offline operation.
4. **Persistence and authority need application behavior.** IndexedDB commits, audit/revision/queue atomicity, receiver acknowledgement persistence, replay-safe receipts, approved-only routing and pause/stop recovery are absent from toolkit. Local peer simulation must be labeled, with queued/transferred/acknowledged/filed distinct. No real identity, endpoint, transport retention policy or encryption is validated. Do not call an actor string authentication.
5. **Complete named outputs matter.** Both Track A document families and Track B verified action list, draft status updates, filing manifest, reviewed routing package, sources, audit and one-page path to fleet must remain in the workflow. Fixture has six proposed actions, four verified tasks, unresolved A-04/A-06, and six unsubmitted draft updates. Dependency-pending A-05 is not completed by review. Accessibility, narrow layout and keyboard/error recovery remain necessary.
6. **Benefits are synthetic.** Fixture's 24 versus 9 minutes over eight packets yields 120 minutes / 2 hours; measuredPrototypeMinutes is null. Seeded-error denominator is five invalid plus 25 valid fields: expected detection 5/5, false positives 0/25, zero remaining errors after correction. Those are expected fixture values, not observed accuracy/time savings or panel scores.
