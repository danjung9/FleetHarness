Research date: 2026-11-04. Phase: research only. Data boundary: **synthetic, not validated against the real format**. This report contains reuse recommendations and unresolved questions, not an implementation plan, architecture contract or application verification. `requirements.md`, `scope.md` and `.fleet/` remain unchanged.

## Findings and decision boundary

Provisionally **adopt IndexedDB** as the browser-local persistence candidate and **adapt the supplied reconciliation and upload-planning primitives** for the synthetic demonstration. **Adapt PouchDB/CouchDB as an established-replication evaluation option**, rather than selecting or installing it now. Its candidate rationale is available, but upstream behavior, license, version and compatibility evidence are absent. That is insufficient evidence to endorse it for operational deployment or to declare a bespoke replication protocol preferable.

The scope retains both tracks: handwritten training logs, supply requisition and routine correspondence; and a week of tasking producing a verified action list, draft status updates and records filing manifest. A generic record editor or successful merge calculation cannot satisfy that workflow. All reuse recommendations are conditional on preserving source citations, uncertainty, human review of the exact revision, auditability, restart recovery and explicit conflict review.

No website was fetched, dependency installed, connector exercised, application implemented or test run. Supplied JavaScript was read as source only. No operational endpoint was contacted. Licenses, upstream release versions, runtime compatibility, government-device suitability, encryption and low-code integrations remain unverified.

## Concrete integration risks and options for planning

These are decision constraints and open questions, not a sequence of implementation tasks.

**Persistence and offline loading (UC36-1, OFF-05, EVAL36-4, DELIVERABLE36-3).** IndexedDB data storage does not itself establish that the application shell, extraction assets and source images load disconnected after restart. A locally installed/served bundle and an offline shell cache are options whose runtime behavior remains to be assessed. Retain drafts on quota, transaction or storage failure; never show a false saved state. Device restart is stronger evidence than a pure merge return value. Origin changes, storage clearing/eviction and retention policy need explicit recovery/export treatment. Plain local storage is not device encryption.

**Revision semantics (OFF-01 to 03, UC36-2 to 3).** Source inspection of T shows immutable ID/content collision detection, missing-parent and cross-record-parent rejection, cycle detection, sorted operations, concurrent heads and null tombstones. Inputs have limits of 5,000 revisions per input array, 100 parents and 50,000 characters per value. These are observed source constraints, not tested capacity. Structured domain records need a stable encoding and attachment references rather than binary content in values. Approval must bind to the exact revision, and both edits and merge resolutions must invalidate stale approval. Audit entries must retain reviewed heads, chosen value and reviewer role. Actor strings are not authentication. Clock-based winner selection is unsuitable. Replaying revisions alone does not deduplicate routing side effects.

**Transfer and acknowledgement semantics (OFF-04).** T sorts by descending caller priority, then ascending time and ID, and returns chunks at the supplied offset. It allows up to 2,000 items, positive attachment byte lengths and priorities 0 to 5. Planned `pendingIds` describes what would remain after planned bytes, not proof of acknowledged delivery. Application queue state must follow actual simulated acknowledgements. Zero budget, disconnect after send but before acknowledgement, duplicate acknowledgement and restart with partial progress must preserve pending work. Critical records and photo bytes need consistent accounting. Real authentication, receiver durability, authorization, retention and encrypted transport remain outside the demonstrated link simulation.

**Two complete tracks and evidence (EVAL36-1 to 2, UC36-5 to 6).** No independent answer key, training-log image, realistic complete packet or synthetic filing schedule was supplied. Planning needs literal invented expected results for extraction, correction, classification and each named output, established before implementation. Preserve original source locations beside candidate values and corrections, including ambiguous/missing fields and overlapping assignments. A labeled synthetic extraction strategy may demonstrate the requested behavior but cannot establish handwriting accuracy on real scans. No real messages, personal identities, credentials, signatures, or government records may be introduced. UI, fixtures, exports and evidence must retain “synthetic, not validated against the real format.”

**Platform transition (UC36-4).** Which government browser/device policies, local installation method and owner low-code platform would permit persistence, offline execution, packaging and migration? No answer is supplied. An owner-platform adapter and an established replication stack are concrete later options; neither is authorized for deployment or verified here. No recommendation implies CAC integration, authority to operate or tamper-proof records.

**Human and operational outcomes.** Preserve intake deferrals without expanding them: UC36-4 requires actual device/platform evidence; EVAL36-3 requires a Fleet-SME-established timing baseline; EVAL36-5 requires a Sailor panel's judgment; EVAL36-6 requires official judges; DELIVERABLE36-1 and DELIVERABLE36-2 require measured operational workload/time and matched error/rework data. Synthetic timing, invented baseline comparisons, seeded-error counts and assessment worksheets are stand-ins only. Missing official form mappings or experts cannot justify dropping named synthetic behaviors.
