## Risks, operational requirements and human gates

- Human reviewer compares H source, pre-build key and actual output; records discrepancies and review identity/decision in verification.md without inventing approval. UI acknowledgement is synthetic local review, not independent human ground truth.
- The data boundary prohibits real messages or system access. Future operational assessment needs authorized real-format examples, identified USMTF revision, SME-reviewed mapping and actual shipboard time conventions; none are supplied. No requirement is deferred on the basis of missing expertise. Synthetic behavior must not be promoted to operational validation.
- File-origin localStorage and download behavior require browser evidence. The frozen declarative DSL cannot toggle networking; offline execution requires a separately recorded disconnected-runtime check. If runtime is missing, verification fails rather than borrowing Node/toolkit results.
- Human review assesses lane/bar clarity, source highlight accuracy, narrow layout, keyboard focus, error recovery and usefulness to watchstanders. Browser assertions alone do not prove usability/accessibility or fitness for operations.
- OCR, handwriting recognition, live transport, authentication, production storage and government connections are outside this synthetic plain-text slice; none has been exercised or certified. No external dependency is required.
- Host freezes acceptance-plan.json and ux-spec.json immediately after planning. Build/repair cannot weaken them or convert a failed implemented criterion to a deferral. A suspected expectation defect requires a new planning run. One bounded implementation repair covers Node and browser failures together.
