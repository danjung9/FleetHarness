The local runtime reports Node `v26.0.0`, above the catalog's minimum `22.19`. This checks executable availability only, not target-device compatibility. No installed dependency tree or application exists to validate. `fleet_helpers` returned no reusable helpers or errors; no helper was needed for this read-only research.

Both tracks remain required: completed handwritten training logs, supply requisition document and correspondence package; a verified action list, draft status updates and filed records. Reconciliation alone does not meet the challenge. The synthetic browser workflow also requires source-grounded extraction, uncertainty/correction, validation, exact-revision review, audit, durable offline routing queues, restart recovery, conflict resolution and acknowledged resumable transfers. Source traceability, accessible tablet/narrow interactions and the one-page path to fleet remain obligations.

## Concrete adapter mismatches and risks

1. **Patch operations versus complete values.** Fixture operations have `operationId`, replica, type, timestamp and patch payload. Toolkit accepts `{id,recordId,parents,actor,value}`, with string value or null tombstone. Adapter must preserve original metadata and derive deterministic full revision snapshots from ancestry, retaining unchanged form/source fields. Null represents delete; explicit merge references every reviewed head. Never stringify the patch alone and treat it as the whole record. Never select a branch by timestamp.
2. **Transfer priorities are reversed.** Fixtures use lower number first (requisition 0, photo 2); toolkit sorts higher priority first within integer range 0 to 5. A documented validated mapping is required; direct forwarding violates SC-TRANSFER. Fixture fields `sizeBytes` and `acknowledgedOffset` map to `bytes` and `uploadedBytes`; toolkit additionally requires `createdAt`, absent from transfer fixtures. Planning must state a deterministic synthetic age assumption without altering fixtures. Sending or `completeAfterAck` does not acknowledge bytes. Toolkit `pendingIds` reflects projected post-plan completion, not committed receiver state.
3. **Out-of-order history versus invalid history.** Toolkit rejects missing parents, cross-record ancestry, cycles and immutable-ID collisions. Fixture reversal requires retaining received children pending missing history, then reconciling the complete union. Preserve original queue/state on error, distinguish waiting history from permanently invalid graphs, and do not discard tombstones.
4. **Durable review/route integrity.** Toolkit actor strings do not authenticate reviewers. Approval must bind the exact immutable revision; any new edit, including equivalent content, requires review again. Unresolved conflicts, invalid fields and absent enclosures block routing. Loading REVIEW-01/AUDIT-01 must not perform those human actions. Audit needs durable application transitions, not only computational output.
5. **Offline scope and shared logic.** The catalog client calls a local HTTP backend. External disconnection with that service available is feasible; backend loss and browser restart are separate failure modes. Planning must define availability, offline assets and durable local edits without duplicating computational implementations in UI/test code. A cached shell alone does not supply processing. Embed source fixtures safely, keep hostile MSG-29 text inert, and retain visible synthetic provenance in UI/exports.
6. **Extraction is a required gap.** No OCR engine is included. ART-TRAIN is a rendered SVG with source regions and uncertain `6O`, not a real scan. A disclosed constrained source-region transcription/extraction approach can demonstrate these fixtures, must keep alternatives visible and require human selection of 60. It cannot claim arbitrary handwriting recognition. Normalized form answers and ANSWER-TASKS cannot supply runtime extraction. Text parsers must derive task candidates, explicit amendments/status updates, duplicate citations and unresolved contradictions from source bodies/attachment.
7. **Records and routing are domain work.** SYN-R1 to R4 are invented holds for disposal review, never deletion authority. Unknown categories/open tasks stay on hold. Requisition capture date is context, not extracted content. Locally queued routes and `routed-local-simulation` never establish actual recipient delivery.

## Fixture anchors and downstream evidence needs

Reuse `data/README.md`'s exact grammar and all 23 scenarios. Source inputs include three layouts, training SVG, requisition/correspondence/enclosure, seven dated messages, task attachment, schedule and 4096-byte placeholder attachment. Expected public outcomes include three completed forms; four deduplicated actions, two verified and two needing review; exact status draft from ANSWER-TASKS; source/rule-linked classifications; heads `[OP-A, OP-B, OP-DEL]` before human merge and only OP-MERGE afterward. Source requisition quantity 12 precedes the separate conflict outcome quantity 14.

SC-TRANSFER expects 174 requisition bytes and 850 photo bytes from photo offset 2048 under budget 1024; acknowledged photo offset becomes 2898, then 1198 bytes remain. Lost acknowledgments advance nothing. BENCHMARK-01 assumes `(24−9)×8/60 = 2` estimated hours and seven errors across six independent cases; these are not measured fleet gains. The worksheet remains “Awaiting human assessment” with no invented scores.

Planning must establish complete literal outcomes and criterion mappings before build, including source-to-answer comparisons and boundary inputs beyond public examples. Meaningful application checks need replay/opposite merge order, edit/delete conflicts, missing/corrupt ancestry, stale approval, zero budget/invalid offsets, lost acknowledgment, stop/resume, storage failure, reset cancellation and actual offline reload. Browser journeys must prove both tracks, review and failure recovery at desktop/narrow widths; toolkit fixtures and helper self-tests cannot establish this. Host computational evaluation, browser evidence, visual review/recheck, Sailor review and operational validation remain separate gates. No check has passed in research.

Open integration questions for planning: exact public invocation shape/status/error ordering; deterministic patch/priority/age mappings; local backend availability boundary and shared-core reuse; atomic persisted-state boundary; constrained source extraction with real derivation; safe export/reset and all literal named outputs. These are routine design responsibilities, not missing owner decisions for the synthetic research phase. Real-device encryption, identity, authorized endpoints, low-code platform, official format revisions, representative handwriting, records authority, measured SME baseline and pilot authorization remain future operational dependencies.
