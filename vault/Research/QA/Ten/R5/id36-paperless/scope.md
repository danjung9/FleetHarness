## Scope and source interpretation

Source: Paperless Underway, challenge 36, The Autonomous Shipboard Enterprise; packet snapshot captured 2026-11-04, source https://cdao.hackathon.maximus.com/event/norfolk/use-cases/36. The supplied packet is the source interpretation for this phase; no remote records or sites have been accessed. `requirements.md` remains the authoritative, unchanged host brief. `challenge` and `userScope` both request Track A and Track B; neither is silently narrowed away. “Offline records and synchronization” is the engineering recipe, not a published event requirement.

Audience: expeditionary/underway Sailors capturing forms on tablets, command staff consolidating tasking, reviewers and records clerks. Primary task: turn captured training logs, supply requisitions, correspondence and a week of tasking into validated, reviewed, locally routed packets and a verified action list, draft status updates and correctly classified synthetic records, surviving disconnection and restart.

Smallest complete prototype: one synthetic unit, two local replica identities, one reviewer role, three Track A document types, and a one-week Track B corpus. Source artifacts are ingested and transformed, rather than replaced by a generic task form or prefilled results. Every extracted value links to a source artifact and location; uncertain handwriting or ambiguous tasking goes to human correction. Review happens before submission or routing; editing an approved revision invalidates its approval. Local routing during disconnection means a durable assignment/queue to a synthetic role, not delivery to an unreachable recipient. Reconnection reconciles two local simulated replicas, resolves competing revisions with an explicit human decision, and completes only approved queued routing. UI and exports must distinguish saved, awaiting review, blocked, queued, transferred and routed states.

Observable success: offline capture/extraction, correction, validation, approval and local routing remain usable without network; reload retains data; reconnect/replay does not duplicate submissions; competing edit/delete heads remain recoverable; reviewer resolution converges; resulting action list, status drafts, filing decisions, completed forms and audit history match independently prepared answers. No claim of real cloud delivery or operational readiness follows.
