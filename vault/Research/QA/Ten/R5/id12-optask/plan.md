## Draft, saved and recovery states

LocalStorage separates draft request/text/history and committed saved request/result; reload preserves both and explicitly labels dirty work. Parse captures an immutable request; subsequent edits invalidate its eligibility for save/export. Save review with dirty unparsed text shows the exact frozen error and cannot overwrite the previous committed snapshot. Discard draft deliberately restores saved content. New sample selection with clean state is direct; dirty changes use a visible discard/save confirmation. Storage failures preserve current text and warn that reload may lose it; export offers a recovery artifact. Service errors preserve drafts, report service unavailable and offer Retry. No stale result or canned sample output substitutes for a failed calculation.

External-offline scope is explicit: local-only mode applies a document CSP/connect policy restricting fetch to same-origin loopback and all resources to self, with no external links fetched automatically. Enable it before parsing a new message in the offline journey.
