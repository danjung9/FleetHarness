## Audience, task and successful outcome

Tactical watchstanders and a reviewing analyst need to resolve a vessel supplied by Name, IMO or MMSI and rapidly inspect a concise evidence-backed contact brief. The smallest complete workflow is: enter an identifier; resolve an exact identity or show ambiguous candidates; retrieve only permitted local snapshots across the named maritime sources; generate a one-page brief; inspect each claim's exact source excerpt and timestamps; review conflicts, stale evidence and UNKNOWN values; and print or download the labeled draft for human review. A chat interface alone is insufficient.

Successful demonstration means a new query computes the expected vessel-specific brief from the prepared corpus, every affirmative factual claim has an exact permitted source span, no unsupported field is guessed, ambiguous identities never merge automatically, and citations open the matching preserved snapshot. A concise brief must retain its citations and synthetic label in its printable/exported form. Intent cannot be inferred from presence, port calls or sanctions-name similarity; interpretation must be labeled and traceable, or UNKNOWN.

## Outputs and computational capabilities

Required synthetic outputs are a disambiguated vessel identity or explicit unresolved state; a concise “1-page intelligence brief”; claim-to-source citations with exact excerpts and timestamps; stale/conflicting/missing evidence notices; and a print/download draft retaining source links and provenance. Use `UNKNOWN` consistently for unresolved factual values, satisfying both source spellings “Unknown” and “UNKNOWN”. Include source snapshot capture times separately from underlying event times, and an as-of time based on the declared clock. Brief headings and interpretation are distinct from factual claims; factual text in headings must also be supported.

Central capabilities are deterministic identifier normalization and conservative resolution; ingestion/validation of the invented source-specific snapshots; permission filtering before search; evidence retrieval with stable spans; freshness/conflict evaluation under labeled synthetic rules; and claim-grounded brief assembly with abstention. A lexical search primitive may assist retrieval but cannot alone establish identity, sanction status, grounding or one-page delivery. Model assistance, if later proposed, is limited to checkable interpretation; no external model or operational data use is required for this prototype.

Proposed measurements: 100% citation coverage for affirmative factual claims; 100% exact-span and digest consistency for returned evidence; zero excluded-source results; all declared unsupported/ambiguous cases abstain; all source-family and identifier scenarios match independent expectations; one-page print review on a declared paper size at desktop plus usable narrow-screen viewing. Record corpus size, measurement environment and query latency percentiles over independently held-out queries. No arbitrary production latency target or measured watchstander time saving is asserted.
