## Integration findings and risks

**Identity and sanctions applicability:** the helper searches document text rather than resolving vessels. A same-name hit or numeric substring must not join two identities or establish designation. Scope already proposes explicit fixture identity links, duplicate-name candidates and human selection. Preserve that distinction and unresolved/conflict states. No authoritative identity or sanctions-screening policy is supplied or inferred.

**Citation integrity:** original-text offsets are useful but a snippet around a query term is not automatically the exact supporting span for a claim. The chosen application must preserve captured bytes, digest, stable snapshot identity, claim-specific locator and capture/observation timestamps through viewing and export. Mutation detection in a local fixture is not production tamper-proof storage. Unicode indexing, newline changes and export encoding can invalidate offsets; an FTS adapter must resolve back to the original snapshot rather than reconstructed highlighted text.

**Filtering and trust:** the inspected helper uses an `approved` boolean and rejects future-dated documents from results. This supports a synthetic boundary fixture only. It provides no authenticated permissions or operational access control. Excluded content must also stay out of identity candidates, snippets and exports. Corpus text is untrusted data; embedded instructions cannot change policy or grant permission. Generic diagnostics can count excluded records without revealing their contents.

**Freshness:** the helper uses only `updatedAt`, defaults to 90 days, and returns a stale flag rather than a policy decision. Scope instead proposes a visible fixed synthetic as-of of `2026-11-04T12:00:00Z` and provisional 30 days, with observation and capture age separate. That rule is synthetic and unvalidated. Missing observation times, conflicting observations and historical records require explicit uncertainty; fresh capture does not establish fresh observation.

**Corpus and output completeness:** supplied fixtures cannot represent the whole challenge. New fictional AIS, IMO, OFAC-style, EU-style and port-log records, ambiguous identities and negative cases are needed in later phases. Real-format compatibility remains unverified. The named outputs remain Cited Contact Brief, Evidence Appendix / Snapshot Viewer, Identity Resolution Record and Synthetic Evaluation Report. A concise one-page brief still needs resolvable exported local evidence, UNKNOWN fields and separate interpretation; a search-result list alone would be incomplete.
