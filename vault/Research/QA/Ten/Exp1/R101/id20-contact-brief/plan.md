## Synthetic data contract and fixed assumptions

All fabricated names/identifiers are fixtures even if they coincide with real entities. No IMO checksum or real vessel validation is claimed. IMO is exactly 7 digits, MMSI exactly 9 digits; strip optional IMO label and surrounding spaces, reject mixed characters; name trim/collapse whitespace and uppercase, nonempty. Exact identifier equality joins a fixture vessel; names produce candidates, never merge identities. Conflicting identifiers withhold selection.

Fixed as-of `2026-11-04T12:00:00Z`; stale means age strictly greater than 30×24 hours, calculated independently for capture and observation. Boundary exactly 30 days is fresh. Exclude future capture or known future observation before matching. Unknown observation remains UNKNOWN and cannot silently establish current applicability. Historic evidence can be cited with a STALE label. Equal-time conflicting AIS values withhold location; retain both competing spans for review, never choose by input ordering. Name-only sanctions evidence cannot designate a vessel; a fixture exact IMO-linked designation is evidence of that synthetic record only. Missing source coverage is UNKNOWN, never sanctions clearance.

Snapshot fields: id, revision, family (AIS/IMO registry/OFAC/EU/port logs), locator (synthetic://...), permitted, capturedAt (required valid UTC), observedAt (valid UTC or null), text (original UTF-8 content), expectedDigest, vesselIds, spans. Span fields: id, start, end (zero-based, end-exclusive JavaScript UTF-16 code units), excerpt exactly equal to text.slice(start,end), supported field/value and vesselId or explicit name-only applicability. Compute SHA-256 over UTF-8 text. Document this difference between byte hashing and character locating. No Unicode normalization or newline rewriting of captured text. Facts refer to snapshotId/spanId and cannot use arbitrary unbound values. Freeze loaded structures and check digest at import, restore, citation opening and export. Store original snapshot, not rendered HTML. Apply synthetic permission and temporal filters before any candidate, index or snippet creation. Validate corpus atomically; do not replace good data when one record is malformed.

Brief object: synthetic=true, asOf, query, identity record, fields (value/status/references), gaps, conflict references, source-family coverage, saved interpretation, separate draft, review state bound to committed evidence/note. Unknown fields have no asserted value. Every machine factual field needs at least one verified permitted supporting span. Unsupported/mismatched references fail closed. Generic excluded counts reveal no denied names, text, IDs or locators to users or exports.
