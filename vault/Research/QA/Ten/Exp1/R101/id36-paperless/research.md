Do not combine PouchDB's native revision mechanism with the toolkit DAG on the assumption that they are interchangeable. The concrete options are an IndexedDB-backed local simulation using the supplied pure operations, or a future PouchDB/CouchDB replication adapter after inspecting and exercising its semantics. Reject clock-based last-writer-wins, silently discarding tombstones, marking a send as acknowledged, or using volatile memory as the only saved state: each conflicts with the supplied offline skill and scope. No bespoke production replication protocol is recommended.

## Integration risks and questions for planning

- **Whole workflow and review:** Offline capture/extraction, correction, validation, review, local routing and all named exports must remain possible in both tracks. What immutable snapshot binds the review decision, source citations and attachments? What prevents a retry from producing a second routing effect? Unresolved heads and edits after approval must block routing until reviewed again (UC36-1/2/3/6, OFF-01/02/05).
- **Offline availability versus saved data:** IndexedDB does not itself provide an offline application shell. Determine the local provisioning and asset availability assumptions. Failed writes must preserve the draft and avoid a saved indicator. Browser reload evidence does not prove a physical-device restart, eviction resistance, device encryption or government-device approval (UC36-4, OFF-05).
- **Revision integrity and recovery:** Missing parents, cross-record parents, cycles and ID/content collisions must preserve the queue and expose a recoverable error. Source history growth may exceed primitive limits. Resolution must include every reviewed head, including deletes; concurrent changes arriving during review require renewed review (OFF-01/02/03).
- **Receipts and transfer:** The scope's 12,288-byte image starts at 6,144 acknowledged bytes. A 4,096-byte budget allocates 1,024 to the critical record and 3,072 to the image, reaching 9,216; another 3,072 completes it. These are specified synthetic expectations, not a research measurement. Distinguish planned, sent and acknowledged bytes, including interruption after send but before acknowledgement. Who defines real priorities, integrity checks and receiver receipts? (OFF-04).
- **Evidence and metrics:** Preserve independent synthetic answer keys, ambiguity and seeded errors rather than deriving expected values from output. Distinguish raw extraction from reviewer-corrected accuracy. Timing, assumed-baseline arithmetic and fictional volume estimates cannot establish actual returned hours. Sailor and judging worksheets stay unscored until authorized human evaluation (EVAL36-1 to 6, DELIVERABLE36-1 to 3).
- **Platform and security:** Which browser/storage policies, low-code runtime, identity, release authority, encryption/key lifecycle, attachment limits and retention rules would an owner authorize? Local role names are not authentication; local logs are inspectable but not tamper-proof. No operational connector is authorized or exercised. Future transition needs monitoring, escalation and shutdown ownership as well as an endpoint.

These are handoff questions and constraints, not a sequencing plan. Missing official formats or SMEs do not justify dropping synthetic work. No `plan.md`, acceptance contract, UX contract or application was created in research.
