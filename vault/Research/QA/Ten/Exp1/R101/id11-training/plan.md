## Data contracts and synthetic assumptions

One epoch: 2026-11-09T00:00 on a fixed ship-local clock. Minutes are integers relative to this epoch, including negative prior duty and dates after the displayed horizon. Display week [0,10080), Monday 09 through Sunday 15 November. Half-open assignment intervals; deadlines permit end equal to deadline. Daily accounting splits intervals at midnight. No timezone, DST, actual policy or format compliance claim.

Application records (internal shapes are builder-owned; these semantic fields are mandatory):

- Workspace: schemaVersion, synthetic=true, epoch, horizon, draft, committed input revision, policy revision, board revision, last valid result, pending draft, review, search budget/status and logs. Immutable snapshots tie all named reports to one committed revision.
- Person: stable ID, fictional name/department, qualifications with validity intervals, explicit availability, priorDuty intervals. Qualification must cover the full assignment; required role counts use distinct people. Person IDs in scope C01 to C06 and exact names/qualifications are retained. No inferred qualification or completion history.
- Resource: stable ID/name and explicit available windows; capacity is exclusive. Operations: weekday training windows 08:00 to 17:00, Flight Deck additionally closed Monday before 13:00. Validate both resource and operations calendars.
- Request: drill/department/participant, duration, role counts, preferred start/person/resource, allowed windows, candidate starts, candidate crew/resource allocations, recurrence anchor/interval/history, priority. Keep candidate alternatives explicit and bounded, not arbitrary real-valued optimization.
- Occurrence: stable drill occurrence ID, release, deadline, duration, request ID, history coverage or assignment, overdue/uncovered state. Account for every required occurrence even outside the visible week. All required participants' requests are mandatory, never optional optimization casualties.
- Policy: version, synthetic warning, daily=240 minutes, weekly=720, minimum inter-block gap=720, maximum block span=480, recurrence and change reason. Form a person's blocks by merging assignments/prior duty with successive gaps less than 720 minutes; gap equal to 720 starts a new block. Every merged block spans at most 480. Prior duty is included; daily/weekly budgets count actual duty minutes, not idle time inside a block. Weekly accounting uses anchored seven-day cycles, including next week's boundary occurrence. Inter-block and block-span policies must be checked together, not toolkit restMinutes substitutions.
- Search result: invalid_input, feasible, infeasible, budget_exhausted or stopped; visited nodes/budget, complete assignments only on feasible, observed rejected candidates and reasons. Infeasible means exhaustive over the supplied candidate set; exhaustion is unresolved. Never describe diagnostics as a minimal unsatisfiable core.
- Review: exact board and policy revision, alias, deliberate confirmation, notes and synthetic acceptance state. Export uses pb4t-synthetic-review.txt, readable UTF-8 with all six named outputs, stable occurrence IDs, assignments, per-day/week loads, search status, revisions, policy, review record and synthetic warning. Raw JSON stays secondary.
