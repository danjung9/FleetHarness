# Fielding brief: permissions, classification, messaging and prerequisites

**No classified data. No authenticated identities. No Navy messaging connection. No authority to operate.**

**Purpose.** Synthetic CASREP drafting and sequential command review on one browser, including return/revise, competing amendments and local release exports. Not validated against real CASREP format or operational rules.

**Permissions.** Sailor/LPO drafts and submits. Division Officer, Department Head, Engineer Officer/CHENG and XO approve in order; CO explicitly simulates release. Current holder may return with a reason; all simulated roles may comment. Content revisions invalidate approvals. Historical revisions and returns remain. Role names and local timestamps are unauthenticated; this is not secure multiuser access control.

**Classification and records.** Fictional inputs only. Local storage is unencrypted, editable and browser-dependent. Real handling needs classification review, approved devices, identity/delegation, access controls, retention/disposal, backup and tamper-resistant audit. The sponsor must determine classification boundaries before real data enters any system.

**Messaging and lifecycle.** Both downloads describe the same immutable simulated release; neither is official transmission. Navy integration needs approved interface specifications, authority, signatures, authenticated transport and receiver acknowledgements. Microsoft 365 connectors, licenses and official release systems are unresolved. Linked UPDATE/CORRECT/CASCOR undergo fresh review; CORRECT supersedes and CASCOR closes only on simulated release. Cancel/expire are explicit local demonstration markers, not Navy policy or automatic deadlines.

**Prerequisites to field.** Operational sponsor and sustainment owner are unresolved. Obtain authoritative NWP 1-03.1 and JSN rules; independently validate fields, routing and lifecycle policy with domain owners. Approve hosting/device installation, offline updates/rollback, security architecture, integration contracts, licensing, training and test measures. Run observed user tasks, interrupted-storage recovery and controlled end-to-end message acknowledgements on approved infrastructure. Success requires sponsor-recorded acceptance and authorization; a local prototype pass supplies neither.

**Monitoring and shutdown.** Local status, validation and audit only. Stop demonstration blocks mutations; downloads remain available. Escalate unresolved questions to the future owner outside the app. No notification or background release runs.
