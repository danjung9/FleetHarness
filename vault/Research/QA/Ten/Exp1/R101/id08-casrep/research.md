## Concrete risks and questions for planning

- **UC8-1, formatting and permissions:** Scope provides fictional categories 2/3/4, required fields and linked-report rules for UPDATE/CORRECT/CASCOR. Reusing generic forms must preserve this CASREP vocabulary and message preview while visibly stating that real NWP rules are unvalidated. Simulated role selection is not authentication. Which future authority will validate format editions, category semantics, delegation and actual release policy?
- **WFL-01, stale approval and state changes:** A merge helper knows nothing about routing or approval validity. Content edits, including conflict resolution, must invalidate previous approvals; comments alone must not. A released snapshot must remain immutable while a linked new draft routes afresh. A user-entered actor and local timestamp do not authenticate a decision. What approval digest and event schema will planning choose to bind decisions unambiguously to content?
- **WFL-02, lineage and data loss:** Reconciliation must not silently select by clocks or discard a competing amendment. Missing history, edit/delete conflicts, ID collisions, oversize history and serialization errors are concrete integration risks. Local save failures must not show success. How will the later design recover from quota/corruption without replacing the last valid record? Real distributed concurrency is outside this scope.
- **WFL-03, reviewer evidence:** A complete return/revise/export walkthrough and human review remain necessary; fixture results cannot establish them. Later application tests need to exercise actual transition and persistence functions, and host browser journeys must cover desktop and narrow interactions including reload. Human acceptance is separate from generated scripts and passing automation.
- **DELIVERABLE8-1, release and outputs:** Preserve `synthetic-casrep-message.txt`, `synthetic-casrep-history.json`, the live-walkthrough script and one-page fielding brief as named outputs. The exports must agree on the final record/revision and retain both competing amendments and decisions. Local simulated CO release must never imply official transmission. Formal channel APIs, receiver acknowledgments and Microsoft 365 interfaces remain unconfigured and unexercised.
- **Lifecycle coverage:** The workflow skill raises amendment, cancellation, expiry and supersession. Scope explicitly requires linked drafts after release and linked report types, but supplies no authoritative cancellation/expiry policies. Planning should make synthetic lifecycle assumptions and unresolved real rules visible without inventing Navy policy or silently expanding the operational scope.
- **Classification and fielding:** Use only fictional equipment, ship and actor data. No classified-system adapter, credentials, real personnel information or operational telemetry is authorized. A future owner must resolve classification handling, security approval, identity, retention, licensing and deployment independently. Neither local storage nor exported history establishes encryption, tamper resistance or authority to operate.

These are handoff questions and risks, not blockers to the complete synthetic workflow and not a sequenced implementation plan. The planner owns literal expected outcomes and frozen acceptance/UX contracts. Future verification must distinguish generated artifacts, runnable behavior, tested behavior, human review and operational validation.
