## What the designs close, and what they do not

[E] Note 04 has the clearest mapping to H1 and E1 through E5: canonical manifests, frozen candidate identity, host-owned evidence, locked delivery, temporary staging, final checks and explicit unsupported-entry rejection. [R] Tighten its implementation criteria for digest migration, complete traversal before filtering, and authoritative metadata reconstruction. Those details determine whether a smaller patch actually fixes the reported cases.

[E] Notes 01/02 define a different gate: fixed cases and a checker outside candidate authority. They call for rejecting missing/duplicate/skipped/error cases and deliberately wrong behavior. [R] Keep that gate separate from Node test reporting. A passing independent acceptance record can justify a bounded task claim while V1 remains a product defect, but it must not relabel the defective development-test count as meaningful application assertions.

[E] Executable download already has stronger controls than source export: `electron/main.ts:115-153` reserves a delivery operation, and `electron/executable.ts:65-79` stages only HTML with source checks before/after and a direct byte comparison. [R] Do not weaken these safeguards or claim the E1/E2 source-export reproductions demonstrate the same failure in executable packaging. Both routes still depend on the current source digest and development gate. A ZIP also needs evidence for the trusted wrapper/runtime and the exact produced archive, distinct from accepted HTML identity.

[R] A hash identifies bytes only when the serialized input is unambiguous and complete. It does not establish correct behavior, an unmodified execution environment, independence of the checker, or the human's authority. A folder and a read-only bit under the same unrestricted account do not establish a protected snapshot. Keep these as separate claims and tests.

## Thursday procedure selected by the directive

[E] [[Ops/Directors/Directive-agentic]] already selects manual acceptance of the final copy and defers implementing acceptance stores, checkers, supervisors and snapshots. [R] No new architecture decision is needed for this reconciliation. Use the following as concrete closure criteria for that selected procedure, not as a claim it has been performed:

1. [R] Freeze the partial four-request ID 11 contract from [[Research/QA/Officer/11-Training-Golden]], including the fresh edit, conflict, invalid-input and review/recovery outcomes. Keep the two-request S1 smoke separate. Shared vault cases are public development inputs, not secret holdouts.
2. [R] Obtain observed completion/shutdown of all candidate writers before making the final copy. The readiness HOLD remains until the authorized preparation owner supplies the required evidence. A stopped UI badge alone does not clear this prerequisite.
3. [R] Enumerate the candidate completely, identify unsupported links/dependencies and decide the exact delivery contents. Copy into an evaluator-controlled location. Independently record a properly encoded per-file manifest or the digest of the exact retained package, using a correct scheme rather than FleetHarness's existing ambiguous `sourceHash` as the sole identity.
4. [R] Exercise the fixed cases against the final destination app. Check the actual rendered and exported values, edited input, impossible/invalid cases, review invalidation, save/reopen and the guide's sample steps. The checker must also reject a known wrong result. A passing generated test suite cannot substitute for these observations.
5. [R] Record contract/input version, candidate identity, environment, actual command or manual steps, per-case expected/actual result, remaining limitations and the real review decision. Keep this receipt outside candidate write authority. Use synthetic role labels, and do not invent named approvers or an operational release.
6. [R] Recompute destination identity after checks and preserve those exact bytes. If the deliverable is a standalone ZIP, separately inspect and test the extracted wrapper, persistence and offline claims, then identify the exact archive. Any repackaging or source change requires new relevant checks.
7. [R] Treat missing evidence, changed identity, unresolved incorrect behavior or incomplete shutdown as HOLD. Manual acceptance may establish one checked artifact; it does not close the seven defects in the product, prove all catalog use cases, or authorize operational deployment.

[R] This procedure can compensate for E1/E2 by checking and preserving the exact final copy, for E3 by recording trusted review evidence separately, and for E5 by rejecting unsupported entries or testing the delivered package. It can avoid handing off E4's partial output by using a separately verified destination. It does not improve the product's copy transaction, test count or hash implementation. Those remain open defects until a patch and reproduction prove otherwise.
